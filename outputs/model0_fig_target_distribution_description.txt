GE-79 Target Distribution - Description

Plain-English takeaways:
- Most patients in this dataset had no cognitive impairment: 55 out of 75.
- Fewer patients were impaired: 20 out of 75.
- Because the groups are uneven, accuracy alone can make a model look better than it really is.

Technical interpretation:
- The donut chart shows the binary target distribution used by the models.
- Class 0, No Impairment, contains 55 patients, or 73.3% of the cohort.
- Class 1, Impaired, contains 20 patients, or 26.7% of the cohort.
- This imbalance explains why the majority-class baseline reaches 0.733 accuracy by always predicting No Impairment.
- Macro-F1 and impaired-class recall are emphasized because they reveal whether the model detects the minority impaired group.
- The class distribution is not a model result; it explains the evaluation context for Models 1 and 3.
