Technical interpretation:
- The figure shows the top 12 feature importances from the final Random Forest fit on the locked feature set.
- The highest-ranked variables were fasting_glucose_mg_dl (0.106), glucose_mg_dl (0.104), daytime_sbp (0.096), and global_vasoreactivity (0.096).
- These results suggest the forest relied most on glycemic control, systolic blood pressure, cerebrovascular reactivity, white-matter markers, vascular inflammation, lipids, perfusion, and body mass.
- Feature importance here is impurity-based, so correlated predictors can share or distort importance.
- The values are useful for model interpretation but should not be read as causal effect sizes.
- Because the dataset is small and imbalanced, the importance ranking should be discussed alongside cross-validation metrics, especially impaired recall.
