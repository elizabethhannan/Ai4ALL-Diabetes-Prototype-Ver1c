About This Research Tool

This interactive prototype demonstrates how machine learning models predict Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) in older adults with Type 2 Diabetes using clinical and MRI-derived biomarkers. All predictions are for research and educational purposes only.

Authors & Acknowledgments

⚠️ Research-Only Disclaimer: This tool is not a diagnostic instrument and should not be used for clinical decision-making. Predictions are based on a small research cohort (n=75) and have not been validated on independent data. All results are exploratory and should be interpreted with caution.
